Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. About the Application

Hermes Affiliate Organizer is a personal-use application operated by its owner to organize affiliate products, prepare social media content, manage publishing schedules, and, where authorized, publish content to connected platforms. It is not currently offered to other users.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the application may process affiliate product details, links, photos, videos, captions, publishing schedules, social media account identifiers, and platform access tokens obtained through authorized connections.

3. Purpose of Processing

This information is used to prepare and organize content, manage publishing workflows, connect authorized accounts, and carry out publishing actions requested by the operator.

4. Storage and Retention

Application records are currently stored locally on the operator's MacBook. The application does not currently use a separate online server for its own data storage. Data is retained as needed for the operator's workflows and may be deleted from local application storage when no longer needed. Copies held by connected platforms are subject to their own policies.

5. Third-Party Platforms and Sharing

If platform integrations are enabled, information necessary for authorized functions may be transmitted to platforms such as TikTok. This may include account identifiers, access credentials, and content selected for publishing. Those platforms process information under their respective privacy policies. The application does not currently sell personal information.

6. Security

The operator aims to protect locally stored information and account credentials using reasonable safeguards, including restricting access to the MacBook and connected accounts. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

7. Access, Revocation, and Deletion

The operator can review and remove locally stored application records. Platform account permissions can be revoked through the relevant platform's account settings, where available. Requests or questions about access or deletion can be sent to the contact address below. Revoking a connection may not automatically delete copies already held by a third-party platform.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The effective date will be revised when changes are published.
